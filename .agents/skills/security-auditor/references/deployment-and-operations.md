# Deployment and operations

Start with `.github/workflows`, lockfiles, workspace scripts, `apps/backend/Dockerfile`, `apps/backend/vercel.json`, `compose.yaml`, mobile Expo and EAS configuration, contract build configuration, and database migrations. Verify which manifests actually feed the target environment. Use sanitized inventories, release metadata, configuration summaries and operational records when available.

- Check environment separation for runtime identities, databases, Redis, queues, anchors, SMS, Stellar networks, contract IDs, and secrets. Review fail-closed production configuration, debug/demo routes and account pools, scheduled maintenance, obsolete deployments and callbacks, and public exposure of services. Inspect secret injection and rotation, build-time public variables, log redaction, artifact contents, and history or release scanning through approved read-only evidence.
- Review least privilege, isolation and approval boundaries across cloud, hosting, database, CI, Expo OTA, contract upgrade, DNS, and providers. Check MFA and service identity controls, separation of release and signing authority, privileged audit trails, and offboarding and key revocation procedures.
- Verify build and update provenance for backend, mobile binaries and OTA channels, shared client, and deployed WASM. Check release approvals, artifact and environment binding, signing key ownership, dependency and action pinning, lockfile consistency, dependency risk and scanner coverage, untrusted PR workflow isolation, mutable scripts, promotion and rollback. Inspect live or funding scripts and seed tools for release applicability and authorization boundaries.
- Review production resilience to chain or provider outages, partial migrations, queue backlogs, resource saturation, backup loss, and administrative mistakes. Verify retention and restore evidence, migration and rollback behavior, recovery point and time targets, ledger recovery and reconciliation after rollback or restore, and separation of backups from production privileges.
- Check alert evidence and ownership for treasury discrepancies, abnormal withdrawals or signing, settlement lag, repeated failures, provider drift, administrative changes and secret or key rotation. Review incident procedures and exercise evidence for containing payments, rotating or revoking keys, recovering stuck funds and accounts, preserving audit evidence, and communicating with affected users and providers. Verify available containment measures against the deployed system and account for irrevocable transactions.
